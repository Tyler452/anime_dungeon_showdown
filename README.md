##  Overview
**Anime Dungeon Showdown** is a **2.5D anime-inspired dungeon shooter** combining fast-paced gunplay, melee combat, and magic-based abilities.

Players start in a **small lobby hub** and choose **two characters**, each with distinct weapons and special powers. They then dive into **enemy-filled dungeons** where they must survive, explore, and collect chests containing new weapons and upgrades.

This demo features **two dungeon levels**, allowing players to experience the game’s flow and art direction before full development.

---

### Players can expect:
- Dual-character gameplay and switching system  
- Gun, melee, and magic hybrid combat  
- Randomized dungeon rooms with loot chests  
- Anime-styled VFX, flashy attacks, and vibrant colors

## Demo Structure

- **End Goal:** Survive and escape the dungeon to complete the demo
